Photosynthesis is the process that green plants use to turn sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide into food. Chlorophyll is the green substance in leaves that captures light energy. During photosynthesis, plants release oxygen into the air. The food made by plants is a sugar called glucose. Animals depend on plants because plants produce food and oxygen.

The water cycle describes how water moves between the land, the ocean, and the atmosphere. Heat from the sun causes water to evaporate from lakes, rivers, and the sea. Water vapor rises into the air, cools, and condenses into tiny droplets that form clouds. When the droplets become heavy, they fall as rain, snow, or hail. Some water sinks into the ground and becomes groundwater, while the rest flows across the surface into streams and rivers.

The printing press was an invention that made books faster and cheaper to produce. Before the printing press, people copied books by hand, so books were rare and expensive. Johannes Gutenberg developed a printing press with movable metal type in the fifteenth century. The invention helped ideas spread more quickly across Europe. Wider access to books encouraged reading, education, and the exchange of knowledge.

Learning a language takes regular practice and patience. A learner should read, listen, speak, and write a little every day. New words are easier to remember when they appear in meaningful sentences. Making mistakes is a normal part of learning because mistakes show where understanding can improve. Reviewing old material helps move knowledge into long-term memory. Speaking with another person is one of the fastest ways to improve fluency.

A sentence usually contains a subject and a verb. The subject tells who or what the sentence is about, and the verb tells what the subject does or is. Adjectives describe nouns, while adverbs describe verbs, adjectives, or other adverbs. Clear writing uses short sentences, specific words, and a logical order. A paragraph usually develops one main idea. Reading good writing is one of the best ways to improve your own writing.

The sun is the main source of energy for life on Earth. Sunlight warms the land and the ocean, drives weather, and provides energy for plants. The Earth rotates once each day, which causes day and night. The Earth also travels around the sun once each year, which causes the seasons. The Moon orbits the Earth and affects the ocean tides.

A computer program is a list of instructions that a computer follows. A variable stores a value that can change while the program runs. A function groups instructions so they can be reused. Conditions allow a program to choose between different actions. Loops repeat instructions until a task is complete. Testing helps a programmer find mistakes before other people use the program.
